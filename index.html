A welcoming stay on Negombo Road

Hotel Blue Sky is located at No. 240, Negombo Road, Kurunegala. We offer 25 air-conditioned rooms and the everyday conveniences guests look for: a front desk to help on arrival, a lift, free Wi-Fi and free parking.

Our restaurant serves guests at the table and in their rooms, and our meeting rooms are available for gatherings and business events. Whether you are travelling for work or leisure, our team is here to make your stay comfortable.
